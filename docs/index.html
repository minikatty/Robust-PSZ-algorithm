Robust Personal Sound Zone Control

One scale across both zones

BZ, DZ, reference, and all ten algorithms share one digital safety gain per item. Files are not normalized individually.

Spatial pressure proxy

The two channels are pressures at a horizontal 18-cm bright-zone point pair, not HRTF-rendered binaural signals.

Desired fields

The BZ reference is the desired plane wave, whereas the DZ reference is silence. DZ algorithm tracks expose residual leakage.
